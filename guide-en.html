PRACTICAL GUIDE

Where should a long
product image be split?

A storefront may require several files even when the detail page was designed as one long image. The useful question is not how to make equal-height slices. It is how to avoid text, prices, faces, products, and other important boundaries while keeping every output within the marketplace's current limits.

1. Verify the destination requirements first

Allowed width, file size, and format vary by marketplace and can change. DetailCut's 860 × 5000 defaults are only a starting point. Check the latest seller documentation, then enter the dimensions that apply to your listing.

2. Inspect every suggested boundary

DetailCut looks near each target height for a horizontal row with less visual change. That can help it find whitespace or a quiet background, but it cannot understand the meaning of a sentence or product composition.

Review checklist
  1. No line of text is divided between two files.
  2. No face or important product feature sits on a boundary.
  3. Files 01, 02, and 03 continue in the expected order.
  4. Each file meets the destination's size and width limits.

3. Choose smart or fixed-height cuts deliberately

Use quieter-seam search when the design has visible space between sections. Turn it off when the source was already designed in exact-height units or every output must have a consistent height.

4. Keep the editable original

The exported slices are JPG files, so transparency and editing layers are not preserved. Keep the original PSD, PNG, or long JPG separately and treat the DetailCut ZIP as a publishing copy.
